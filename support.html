Support

Wobble · Party Games Offline

Wobble runs entirely offline. If something looks wrong, it is almost never the connection — start here.

I bought the unlock and things are still locked

Open Settings → Restore purchases. This is the one action in the app that needs the internet: it asks the App Store what your Apple ID owns. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID that made the purchase.

Can I play without internet?

Yes — every game, every deck, start to finish, in airplane mode. The app only reaches the network to check for content updates and to confirm a purchase.

The app is in the wrong language

Open Settings → Language. “System” follows your phone; English, Русский and Українська can also be picked directly. Changing it takes effect immediately.

The narrator is silent, or reads in the wrong voice

Mafia, Detective and Quest are narrated by the phone. The switch is in the setup sheet that opens when you press Play. If your phone has no installed voice for the language you are playing in, the app shows the host’s lines as text with a button to continue instead — the game still plays normally. Voices are installed in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

What do I get for the one-time purchase?

Every game is free to play with its base deck. The unlock adds the extra themed decks in every game, the optional Mafia roles (the Godfather, the Advocate, the Temptress), Detective and Quest, and everything added later. One payment, no subscription.

How do I delete what the app has stored?

Delete the app. Everything it keeps — settings, deck choices, the record of the unlock — lives on the device and goes with it. Nothing about you is stored anywhere else; see the privacy policy.

Something is broken

Tell us, and please include: which game, the language you were playing in, your iOS version, and what happened right before it went wrong. Screenshots help more than anything.

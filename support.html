Support

Wobl · Party Games Offline

Wobl runs entirely offline. If something looks wrong, it is almost never the connection — start here.

I bought the unlock and things are still locked

Open Settings → Restore purchases. This is the one action in the app that needs the internet: it asks the App Store what your Apple ID owns. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID that made the purchase.

Can I play without internet?

Yes — every game, every deck, start to finish, in airplane mode. The app only reaches the network to check for content updates, to confirm a purchase, and to send feedback when you choose to write to us.

The app is in the wrong language

Open Settings → Language. “System” follows your phone; any of the app’s 34 languages can also be picked directly. Changing it takes effect immediately.

The narrator is silent, or reads in the wrong voice

Mafia, Detective and Quest are narrated by the phone. The switch is on the setup page that opens when you press Play. If your phone has no installed voice for the language you are playing in, the app shows the host’s lines as text with a button to continue instead — the game still plays normally. Voices are installed in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

What do I get for the one-time purchase?

Every game is free to play with its base deck. The unlock adds the extra themed decks in every game, the optional Mafia roles (the Godfather, the Advocate, the Temptress), Detective and Quest, and everything added later. One payment, no subscription.

How do I delete what the app has stored?

Delete the app. Everything it keeps — settings, deck choices, the record of the unlock — lives on the device and goes with it. Nothing about you is stored anywhere else. If you sent feedback from the app, it is kept without your name for 180 days and then deleted; to have it deleted sooner, see the privacy policy.

Something is broken

Tell us — by email below if you would like an answer, or with Send feedback at the end of a game if you don’t need one. Please include: which game, the language you were playing in, your iOS version, and what happened right before it went wrong. Screenshots help more than anything.

Privacy Policy

Wobl: Romantic Games — last updated 20 October 2026

Wobl: Romantic Games collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. What you and your partner type and answer stays on your phone.

The rest of this page explains exactly what the app keeps, what it sends and to whom, because “we collect nothing” is easy to write and worth being specific about.

What stays on your phone

Everything the app remembers is stored locally on the device and never leaves it:

  • the two first names you enter at the start, so the cards can address each of you;
  • your settings — language, theme, chosen level and game modes, haptics;
  • the shuffle state that keeps a card from repeating too soon, and a note of which free trial rounds have been played;
  • a note of whether a subscription or the lifetime purchase is active, so the app works offline;
  • a random identifier the app generates the first time you open the purchase screen, for example wbr-3f2ab19c…. It is created on the device, it is not derived from anything about you or your phone, and its only job is to tell the purchase service which install bought access.

Your answers in the games — which card you picked, how you voted, what you guessed — are held in memory for that round and are not written anywhere. Nothing you answer is ever sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of the above with it.

What leaves your phone

The games themselves are fully offline: you can play the entire app in airplane mode. Two technical connections exist, and neither carries anything personal or anything about how you play.

1. Purchases (Apple, and RevenueCat)

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, email, Apple Account or card details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether this device has access. Once you have opened the purchase screen, RevenueCat receives, then and on later app launches:

  • the random identifier described above;
  • the purchase receipt Apple issues, and the status of the subscription or purchase;
  • standard technical details that come with any internet request — IP address, device model, operating system version, app version, country.

RevenueCat does not receive your name, your partner’s name, your email address, your Apple Account or anything you answered in a game, and we do not link that identifier to any person. If you never open the purchase screen, nothing is sent.

2. Content updates (Expo)

New cards and fixes are delivered as content updates rather than full app releases. On launch the app asks Expo’s update service whether a newer version of the app’s content exists. That request carries the technical details any internet request carries — IP address, platform, app and runtime version — and nothing else. No identifier the app created is sent with it. See Expo’s privacy policy.

What we never do

  • No analytics or telemetry of any kind. There is no analytics library in the app.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to track.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email collection.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of data. There is no data to sell.
  • No microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location access. The app asks for none of them.

Age

Wobl: Romantic Games is for adults aged 18 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age; the names you enter never leave the device.

Your data, and removing it

To remove everything the app has stored, including both names, delete the app from your device. To change the names, open Settings in the app. If you would like the purchase record held by RevenueCat removed, write to us via Support and we will ask them to delete it — note that this may prevent restoring the purchase later. Cancelling a subscription is done in your Apple Account settings, not by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a future version of the app ever starts collecting something, that will be said here plainly, before it ships.
